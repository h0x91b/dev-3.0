Short: Long port lists fold behind +N

An Active Tasks row with more than six listening ports now shows the first five plus a "+N more" toggle that expands to every port and collapses back with "Show fewer", so a big test suite no longer turns one task into a sidebar-tall column of badges; the board card's ports popover now scrolls instead of running off the screen. Port badges stay clickable to open in the browser, and neither the badges nor the toggle open the task. Reported by Akvile Ruginyte.
