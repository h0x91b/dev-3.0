Short: Codex starts without update interruptions

Codex launched by dev3 skips its startup update check and menu, including scratch tasks and resumed sessions, so automatic input cannot select an update and leave the pane in a shell. Update Codex manually with `codex update` between sessions; launches outside dev3 keep their own update settings.
