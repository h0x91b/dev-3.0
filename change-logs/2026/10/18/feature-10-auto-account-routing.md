Short: Auto picks the least-used account

Launches can now use "Auto (balanced)" as their account: dev3 picks among your Claude Code or Codex subscription logins by their current 5-hour usage, then by the weekly quota that would otherwise expire unused, reserves room for launches started together so a batch spreads, and never uses API profiles. Make Auto the default from the usage flyout or Settings → Accounts, keep a login away from one project in Project Settings → Board, and when no subscription login has room the launch waits for the next reset instead.
