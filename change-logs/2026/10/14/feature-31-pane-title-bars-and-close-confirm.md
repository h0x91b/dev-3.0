Short: Pane title bars and close confirmation

Settings → Terminal gains two opt-in toggles: "Pane title bars" puts a slanted title tab above every tmux pane, in the style of the window tabs, with its number (green while focused), a zoom marker and an auto-name (the title a program set, else folder │ command), and "Confirm before closing a pane" makes the close-pane shortcut ask "Close pane N?" first, answered with Enter or Y and cancelled with Esc or N; the close-pane shortcut on native tasks now also closes the pane you are actually in.
