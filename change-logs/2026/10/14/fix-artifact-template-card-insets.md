Short: Artifact text no longer hugs card edges

Paragraphs, lists and charts placed beside a table in a dev3 artifact table card now keep the panel inset instead of sticking to the card edge, ledger columns line up with the panel heading, cards no longer add a stray top gap above a leading paragraph, and stacked narrow-screen tables lose their leftover column rules. The chart guide also warns that a legend over a named y-axis needs `grid.top` of at least 56.
