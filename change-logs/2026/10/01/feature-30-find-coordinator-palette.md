Short: Coordinator finder; Go to Project is ⇧⌘K

The ⇧⌘P command palette has a new "Find coordinator…" command that lists every active coordinator task across all projects, marks the current, disconnected, hibernated and hidden ones, and opens the chosen one without starting or waking anything. Go to Project moves from ⌘K to ⇧⌘K (Ctrl+Shift+K on Linux and Windows), leaving plain ⌘K free for the terminal; a shortcut you rebound yourself is kept.
