Short: Each Claude pane resumes its own conversation

After a restart, a task with several Claude panes now brings every pane back on its own conversation instead of reopening one conversation in most of them. Each pane's saved conversation follows `/clear` and `/resume`, and a pane with nothing left to reopen starts a fresh session.
