Short: Relayed grants keep their scope

A coordinator now relays the user's permission to a child task with its scope — the user's words, the operation, exact targets, limits and the child's seq — instead of being told its relay authorises nothing, and the auto-mode classifier clears only what that scope states. Creating a new branch or ref at an exact SHA, an expected-absent `--force-with-lease=<ref>:`, and a non-force landing on the default branch the user asked for are treated as an ordinary push rather than Modify Shared Resources.
