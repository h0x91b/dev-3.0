Short: Icons tell header resource pills apart

The memory, CPU and disk pills in the header now each carry their own icon, and the same icons lead the Memory, CPU and Disk rows in the ⋯ menu and the phone sheet, so the two GB readouts are no longer mistaken for each other, and the CPU and disk breakdowns are titled CPU and Disk instead of reusing the memory panel's "System" heading. The disk breakdown also shows how full the volume is as a percentage, where rounding used to print "2.0 TB of 2.0 TB in use" with space still free.
