Short: Pi joins the harness picker

Plain Pi (earendil-works/pi) is now a first-class harness next to Oh My Pi: it receives the dev3 protocol, resumes the exact session after a restart, moves its task between columns on its own through a generated status extension, and a missing `pi` blocks the launch with the official installer. Oh My Pi is unchanged.
