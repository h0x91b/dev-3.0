Short: Cost tracking for OpenCode, pi, omp

Productivity Stats now also counts tokens and API-equivalent cost from OpenCode (every model call, not only a message's last step), pi and Oh My Pi, including managed pi/omp accounts, and keeps that history after their sessions are deleted. Usage those tools never stored, custom harnesses and logs deleted before the first scan cannot be recovered.
