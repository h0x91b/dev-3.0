Short: Rename terminal tabs in place

Terminal tabs above a task's terminal can now be renamed in place: double-click a tab, click the pencil that appears on hover, press F2 on a focused tab, or pick Rename from its right-click menu — Enter saves, Esc cancels, and an empty name brings the automatic one back. The name sticks: no program's title overwrites it, it survives reconnects and app restarts, and the tmux status line shows the same name.
