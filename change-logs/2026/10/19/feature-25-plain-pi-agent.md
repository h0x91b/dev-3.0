Short: Pi joins the harness picker

Plain Pi (earendil-works/pi) is now a first-class harness next to Oh My Pi: it receives the dev3 protocol, resumes the exact session after a restart and moves its task between columns on its own, and a missing `pi` blocks the launch with the official installer plus, on macOS and Linux, the Homebrew formula. Settings → Accounts holds any number of Pi accounts, each signed in through Pi's own `/login` with any OAuth or API-key provider, picked per launch with that account's models. Also fixes the launch dialog's harness row going blank in a browser when the not-installed notice appeared.
