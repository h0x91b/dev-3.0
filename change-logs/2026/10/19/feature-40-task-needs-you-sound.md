Short: Chime when a task needs you

New opt-in "Task needs you sound" (Settings → Notifications) plays a short chime when a task moves to Your Review or Has Questions, including moves made by agent hooks and the CLI. It has its own toggle, off by default, so the existing completion chime keeps today's behavior.
