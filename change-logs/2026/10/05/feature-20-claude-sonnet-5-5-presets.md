Short: Claude Sonnet 5.5 presets

Claude Sonnet 5.5 (`claude-sonnet-5-5`, native 1M context) gets its own launch presets — Auto and Bypass at Medium/X-High effort plus Default, Plan and Accept Edits — listed directly above Sonnet 5 in every mode group, so it can be picked before launch instead of switching with `/model` inside Claude Code. Claude's default preset is unchanged, and usage costs price it at Sonnet 5's $2/$10 per million tokens with $0.20 cache reads.
