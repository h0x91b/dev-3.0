Short: Pick or customize the artifact template

Settings → Tasks & Board → "Artifact template" now chooses what agents build HTML reports from: the dev3 template, free-form HTML with no dev3 branding, or "My template" — your own folder, seeded with a copy of the dev3 template so you can restyle it and rewrite its AUTHORING.md. Project Settings → Board can override it per project, and asking for a different style for one artifact still works. Free-form pages also keep their own page background and margins instead of having the dev3 fallback theme override them.
