Short: tmux window bar on top, only when needed

The tmux window bar now sits at the top of every task terminal and only appears while the session has more than one window, so a lone window gets its row back and a window opened by Cmd+T or by an agent shows up where the eye starts instead of looking like the task was lost. Live sessions pick it up without a restart, and Alt-click cursor moves account for the bar's new position.
