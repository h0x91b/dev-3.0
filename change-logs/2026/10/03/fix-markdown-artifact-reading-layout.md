Short: Markdown artifacts get a proper reading layout

Markdown reports published with `dev3 show-artifact` now render with a readable text column, clear heading hierarchy, bordered tables and code blocks that may widen past the text and scroll on their own, styled lists, task checkboxes and quotes, in both themes and on narrow screens. A Markdown or HTML report that merely mentions the `data-dev3-artifact-shell` attribute no longer loses its dark/light theme. Already-published artifacts keep their old look until re-published.
