Short: Markdown artifacts get a proper reading layout

Markdown reports published with `dev3 show-artifact` now render with a readable text column, clear heading hierarchy, striped tables with a row hover, code blocks whose long lines wrap under their own indent (box-drawing diagrams keep their shape and scroll instead), styled task checkboxes, quotes and links with hover and keyboard-focus states, in both themes and on narrow screens. They also support GitHub-style callouts (`> [!NOTE]`, `[!TIP]`, `[!IMPORTANT]`, `[!WARNING]`, `[!CAUTION]`), collapsible `<details>`/`<summary>` sections, footnotes and reference links, and headings get GitHub-style ids so `[Section](#section)` links jump to them. A Markdown or HTML report that merely mentions the `data-dev3-artifact-shell` attribute no longer loses its dark/light theme. Already-published artifacts keep their old look until re-published.
