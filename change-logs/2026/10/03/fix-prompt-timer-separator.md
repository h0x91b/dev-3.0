Short: Prompt separators sit flush with segments

The separator in front of the shell prompt's timer segment no longer turns into a dark, flat notch in dev3's dark terminal: segment separators are now drawn without a white background, which the terminal repaints dark. In Settings → Terminal the prompt previews no longer show a small step where each angled separator meets its segment, and no longer squeeze every style down to its narrowest form when dev3 was started from a narrow terminal.
