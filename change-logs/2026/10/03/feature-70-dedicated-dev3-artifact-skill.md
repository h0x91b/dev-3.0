Short: A /dev3-artifact skill for reports

Agents now get a dedicated `/dev3-artifact` skill next to the dev3 protocol: the existing in-app report workflow (copy the task's starter, follow its AUTHORING.md, edit index.html and report.js, publish or version with `dev3 show-artifact`) in one discoverable place, with the boundary to `/dev3-share-artifact` spelled out. The artifact template, its output and the CLI are unchanged, and the protocol keeps its artifact section with a one-line pointer to the skill.
