Short: Tap terminal links on phones

Terminal links can now be opened from a phone or tablet: tapping an agent's hyperlink, a plain URL, a dev3 link or a file path opens a sheet that shows the full destination with Open and Copy buttons, instead of doing nothing because touch has no Cmd/Ctrl key. Scrolling, drag-selection and pane swipes never open it, and desktop Cmd/Ctrl+Click is unchanged.
