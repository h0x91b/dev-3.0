Short: Turn the dev3 artifact template off

Settings → Tasks → "Use the dev3 artifact template" lets agents build free-form HTML artifacts without the dev3 logo, header, footer or colors, Project Settings → Board can override it per project, and asking for one artifact without the template works without changing either setting. Free-form pages now keep their own page background and margins instead of having the dev3 fallback theme override them.
