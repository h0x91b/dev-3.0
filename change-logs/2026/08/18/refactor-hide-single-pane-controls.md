The pane toolbar in the task inspector now hides the layout, zoom and close-pane buttons until the task actually has a second pane, leaving only split, new window and the shortcuts cheat sheet on a single-pane terminal. The header's Pull button follows the same rule: it shows up only when the project's main or master is really behind origin, and disappears again once there is nothing to pull, instead of sitting in the bar greyed out. The No Sleep toggle moved out of the header bar entirely and now lives as a row in the kebab ("More") menu — it is on for everyone and practically never switched off.
