Short: Hebrew and Arabic read correctly

Terminal panes can now reorder right-to-left text (Hebrew, Arabic, Persian) so it reads correctly instead of appearing reversed, via a new Advanced Experience group in Settings → System that hosts beta behaviour and ships off by default. The reorder is display-only: copied text keeps its logical order, while mouse selection and link hover on those lines still follow it, and Arabic letters are not joined into cursive forms — the terminal engine has no right-to-left support of its own yet.
