Added a custom `dev3://` URL scheme (macOS) so links can open the app directly: `dev3://task/<id>` jumps to a task, `dev3://project/<id>` opens a board, and `dev3://new-task?project=<id>&text=<…>` opens the Create Task modal prefilled with text. Right-click a task card → "Copy Deep Link" to grab its link. Requires the app installed in /Applications for macOS to register the scheme.
