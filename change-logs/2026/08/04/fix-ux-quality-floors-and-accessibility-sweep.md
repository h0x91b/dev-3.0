Short: Keyboard focus rings and zoom restored

The keyboard focus ring was invisible on 27 controls: Tailwind's focus variant of
outline-none out-specifies the app's global :focus-visible rule and painted a
transparent outline over it. Pinch-zoom was also blocked in browser and mobile remote
mode, destructive dialogs confirmed with a bare "OK", and several tips pointed at a
Settings category that no longer exists. The UX bible gains a "Quality floors" chapter
— accessibility, contrast, typography, copy, motion and layout thresholds every surface
must clear — and each floor ships with the mechanism and the test that keep it true.
