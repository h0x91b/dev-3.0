Short: Keyboard focus rings are visible again

The keyboard focus ring was invisible on 27 controls: Tailwind's focus variant of
outline-none out-specifies the app's global :focus-visible rule and painted a
transparent outline over it. Destructive dialogs also confirmed with a bare "OK", several
tips pointed at a Settings category that no longer exists, and fixed-width overlays ran
off the edge of a phone. The UX bible gains a "Quality floors" chapter — accessibility,
contrast, typography, copy, motion and layout thresholds every surface must clear — and
each floor ships with the mechanism and the test that keep it true.
