Short: Artifacts get gold and a color palette

Artifact reports are no longer two colors on a dark page: the `DEV3 ARTIFACT` line is now a gold chip, panel headings carry a colored marker, KPI cards and toned panels wear their own hue on rail and edge, and a faint colored wash sits behind the whole page. Six categorical `--dev3-viz-*` hues replace the semantic colors ECharts used as its default series palette, so a chart no longer claims a warning or a failure purely from series order, and authors color a panel with a `tone-1` … `tone-6` class instead of writing a hex. Gold ships as `--dev3-gold` / `--dev3-gold-ink` / `--dev3-on-gold` because a gold that passes contrast as text on a white page is brown; dark-theme neutrals were retuned so table headings finally pass AA contrast.
