Added anonymous PostHog product analytics to the renderer, covering project creation, task create/edit/move/spawn, and branch actions such as rebase, commit, push, PR creation and merge, plus unhandled error and promise-rejection capture. No user identification and no PII is sent; without a build-time `VITE_POSTHOG_KEY`/`VITE_POSTHOG_HOST` the client falls back to a no-op so nothing is captured.
