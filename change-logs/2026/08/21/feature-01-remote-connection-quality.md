Short: See your remote connection speed

Viewing dev3 over remote access now measures the connection's round-trip time and keeps it as a `Connection` row in the header's overflow menu — hover it for the spread, the jitter, lost samples, and how much of the wait was spent on this computer versus on the network. When the link stops behaving (past 150 ms, or jittery enough to feel) the number climbs into the header itself, in the slot the QR icon uses on the desktop, and leaves again when it recovers. If the connection is open but nothing comes back at all, it shows a dash in red rather than disappearing. The number is a real request through the same connection the app uses, sampled every five seconds while the tab is visible. On a machine whose VPN exits far away, that wait is mostly the VPN: the new opt-in `DEV3_CLOUDFLARED_EDGE_BIND=en0` lets the Cloudflare tunnel dial the edge from a chosen interface instead, which moved one measured round trip from 340 ms to 25 ms. It is off unless you set it, because it routes that traffic around your VPN. Help mode also stopped putting its explanation card in the top-left corner of the screen when the control it explains had appeared or disappeared while help mode was open.
