Short: Usage panel fits, and switches again

The header rate-limit panel is about half as tall: each limit window is one dense "label · bar · % · resets in" line, the capture age rides that line instead of taking its own, an account with no reading says so on its headline, and the Account settings button at the bottom became a link in the header row. Switching the default account no longer needs a click on the pill first — the rows arm once the pointer has rested in the panel, so you hover, move down and pick. Usage bars inside a card also render full width again in the desktop app, and a long workspace name now elides instead of pushing the plan and Default chips off the right edge.
