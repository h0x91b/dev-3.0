Short: Agent messages no longer interrupt your typing

An incoming `dev3 message` from another task is no longer typed into the agent's input box the moment it arrives, so it can't get mixed into the line you are writing. The whole message — text and Enter — waits for that pane to be quiet for 15 seconds, waits with no deadline at all for as long as you keep typing, and lands immediately when you press Enter on your own line. A burst of messages from several peers still becomes exactly one turn for the receiver.
