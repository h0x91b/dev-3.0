Short: dev-3.0 walks you through task one

The sandbox now comes with a guide instead of an empty board: a small card rings the button you should press, holds back the rest of the screen,, says what it does, and moves along on its own once you press it — from New Task, through the prompt (already filled in with the real rounding bug in the sandbox's prices.js), past the difference between Save and Save & Start, through the Launch dialog, to the agent working in its own terminal and the diff you read before merging. While a step is open the rest of the screen is held back, so the only way on is the way the card describes, and the card's button presses the real control for you; you can go back at any step, leave with Escape or "I'll look around myself", and if it ever loses sight of what it was pointing at it says so and offers to start over rather than vanishing. It starts again on every visit to an empty sandbox board until you walk it to the end, and after that help mode carries "Walk me through the first task". If no agent CLI is installed — or one is installed but not logged in — the first-run strip now says so and offers Settings instead of a sandbox whose very first task would have died on a login prompt. Help mode also explains two more things it had been silent about: the Project / Space / Global switcher above the task list, and the "Did you know?" card.
