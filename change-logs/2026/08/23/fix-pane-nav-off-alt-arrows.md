Short: Alt+arrows now jump by word

Prefix-free tmux pane switching moved from Alt+arrows to Alt+Shift+arrows, and dev3's managed shells (zsh and bash) now bind Alt+arrows and Ctrl+arrows to word-by-word cursor motion — zsh binds neither out of the box, and macOS claims Ctrl+arrows system-wide for "move a space". `⌃B` + arrows keeps working as before, and the Keyboard Shortcuts overlay's Terminal tab lists the new combo.
