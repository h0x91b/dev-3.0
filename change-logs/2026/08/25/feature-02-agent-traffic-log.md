Short: See who your agents are messaging

Messages your agents send each other with `dev3 message` are no longer just a 30-second toast. While a pair is talking, a small wire glyph appears in the header: it counts live pairs, and opening it shows each one with the task that has not answered yet — click it to land in that task. The full history opens with ⇧⌘M (also View → Agent Traffic Log, or the command palette): every message the agents typed into each other, kept for 30 days, filterable to one pair or to the messages dev3 could not prove reached the other side.
