Short: See who your agents are messaging

Messages your agents send each other with `dev3 message` are no longer just a 30-second toast, once you switch the beta on in Settings → System → Advanced Experience → "Agent traffic readout and log". The overflow (⋯) menu then has an Agent traffic row, and a small wire pill sits next to the ⋯ button for any project whose agents have messaged each other — it carries the count of messages you have not seen yet and drops that number once you look, while the pill itself stays. Both open a panel of live pairs showing which task has not answered yet, and a click lands you in it. The full history opens with ⇧⌘M (also View → Agent Traffic Log, or the command palette): every message the agents typed into each other, kept for 30 days, filterable to one pair or to the messages dev3 could not prove reached the other side. On a phone it all lives in the hamburger, where the log opens as a sheet. Messages are recorded on disk either way, so turning it on later shows the history you already have.
