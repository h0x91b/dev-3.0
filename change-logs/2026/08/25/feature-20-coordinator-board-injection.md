Short: Messages to a coordinator carry the board

Every message dev3 delivers to a coordinator task now ends with a `<dev3-board>` snapshot: each task not parked in To Do, everything finished in the last 24 hours, and how long each one has been sitting in the column it is in. A child reporting in therefore also tells the coordinator what else moved while it was not being spoken to, and the coordinator answers from that instead of spending a turn on `dev3 task list`. A burst of messages carries exactly one snapshot, built as it is typed.
