Short: Turn telemetry off without rebuilding

Telemetry can now be switched off in Settings → System → Telemetry, or from the environment with DEV3_TELEMETRY=off or DO_NOT_TRACK=1 — previously the only way out was a build from source. Switching off is immediate and stops every channel at once, and an opted-out install no longer hands its per-install id to the renderer. Separately, PostHog autocapture now masks element text and attributes so task titles and project names cannot be harvested from the UI, absolute paths are stripped from crash reports before they are sent, exception capture is left to the server-side config instead of being forced on, and session replay plus in-app surveys are pinned off in the client so they cannot be switched on remotely. Found and reported by Mathias Caldas (Screenz mate), whose independent privacy audit of the shipped build prompted all of it — thank you.
