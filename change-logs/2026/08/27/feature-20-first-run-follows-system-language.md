Short: dev-3.0 opens in your language

On a fresh install dev-3.0 now opens in the language your system asks for — Russian, Spanish or English — instead of always English, so you no longer have to find Settings → Appearance → Language before discovering the app is translated at all. Picking a language yourself still wins and is remembered; the automatic guess is not, so it follows your system if you change it. Two Russian and Spanish strings that had been left as their English originals are translated, and a new check keeps that from happening again.
