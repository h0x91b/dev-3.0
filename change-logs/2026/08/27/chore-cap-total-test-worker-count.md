`bun run test` and `bun run test:full` now share one worker budget across their three concurrent vitest processes, instead of each process independently defaulting to `cores - 1` and putting roughly three workers on every core. On a 16-core machine that is 9 workers split 4 mainview / 4 bun / 1 cli instead of 45: the run takes about twice as long, and in exchange the machine stays usable and the CPU-starvation timeouts the backend suite produced on 4 of 5 uncapped runs are gone. A solo `test:bun` / `test:cli`, watch mode, and CI keep the vitest default untouched.
