Short: Hibernate button is legible again

The Hibernate control in the open task's session bar now wears its amber skull glyph instead of a grey outline, so a rare and costly action stops the eye before the tooltip explains it, and hibernating asks for confirmation first — spelling out that the worktree and your changes survive while the terminal scrollback does not, with an extra warning when the task is running several agents at once. Both were authored with the original feature but never reached the merged branch.
