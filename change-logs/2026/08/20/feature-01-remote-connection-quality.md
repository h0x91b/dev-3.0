Short: See your remote connection speed

Viewing dev3 over remote access now shows the connection's round-trip time in the header, in the slot the QR icon uses on the desktop — hover it for the spread, the jitter, lost samples, and how much of the wait was spent on this computer versus on the network. The number is a real request through the same connection the app uses, sampled every five seconds while the tab is visible. On a machine whose VPN exits far away, that wait is mostly the VPN: the new opt-in `DEV3_CLOUDFLARED_EDGE_BIND=en0` lets the Cloudflare tunnel dial the edge from a chosen interface instead, which moved one measured round trip from 340 ms to 25 ms. It is off unless you set it, because it routes that traffic around your VPN.
