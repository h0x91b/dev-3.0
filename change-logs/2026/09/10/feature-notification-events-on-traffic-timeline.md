Short: Notifications join the replay timeline

Recorded `dev3 notify` requests now ride the Agent traffic replay timeline beside messages and board movements, so an hour where only a notification fired still replays step by step. The message list shows each notification's full text with its level, its fate (shown, held back, or never shown because no window was open) and both endpoints exactly as the archive recorded them — an unrecorded sender says so instead of borrowing the target's identity.
