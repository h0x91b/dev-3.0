Short: Native text selection in mobile terminals

Long-press a terminal on a phone or tablet and you now get your platform's own text selection over it — the drag handles and the magnifier — so you can grab exactly the line or path you want, and lifting your finger puts it on the clipboard just like selecting does on the desktop. Previously an iOS long-press highlighted the whole terminal and copied a page archive instead of text, and Android offered no way to copy at all.
