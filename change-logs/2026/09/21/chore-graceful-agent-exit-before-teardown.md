Teardown of a task's terminal now has a step that asks the agent CLI to quit on its own terms first — Ctrl-C, the harness's own `/exit` or `/quit`, then Enter — and waits up to 30 seconds for the pane's process tree to go quiet, so exit-time work such as Claude Code's SessionEnd hooks can finish before the worktree reaper and worktree removal kill it mid-flight. The step stays inert until a launch-scoped readiness receipt can prove a CLI is really at its prompt: it never types at a pane running something other than the agent, never at a CLI whose prompt is unproven (a trust dialog would read the Enter as an answer), and on a platform with no readable process table it logs the skip instead of guessing. No teardown behaves differently yet.
