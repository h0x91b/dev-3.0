Short: Plain JetBrains Mono, honest font names

Settings → Terminal now ships unpatched JetBrains Mono as its own option, and every bundled Nerd Font says so in its name — "Hack Nerd Font", not "Hack". The old labels claimed the upstream typeface while handing over the icon-patched variant, and because a label matching what you typed won the picker, a font you had installed yourself could not be chosen at all. Picking the plain face keeps the Nerd Font at the end of the stack, so prompt and file-listing icons it does not carry still resolve instead of turning into boxes.
