Short: Launch limits with one account

The launch dialog's account line is now always shown for Claude and Codex, instead of only when two or more accounts are registered — so a user with a single login can still read that account's 5h/7d usage while choosing which harness and model to launch. The popover gained an "Add another account" link into Settings → Accounts, switching harness no longer carries an account from the previous one, and closing the popover hands focus back to the pill.
