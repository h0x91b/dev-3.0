Short: Continue-in-another-agent now starts instantly

"+ Agent" with "Continue this task's conversation" now hands the new agent its pointer to the retelling as the launch prompt, so reading it is the agent's first turn. It used to be typed into the pane afterwards and held until the pane went quiet — and because any keystroke anywhere in the task pushes that hold back, the pointer sat unsent until the user pressed Enter in the pane he spawned from.
