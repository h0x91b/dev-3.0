Short: Claude Fable 5.1 presets

Claude Fable 5.1 joins the built-in Claude presets on its 1M-context alias (`claude-fable-5-1[1m]`) with Medium/High/X-High effort tiers for Auto and Bypass plus Default, Plan and Accept Edits, listed above Fable 5 in every mode group. The Fable 5, Opus 5, Opus 4.8, Sonnet 5 and Opus 4.7 presets and the default configuration are unchanged.
