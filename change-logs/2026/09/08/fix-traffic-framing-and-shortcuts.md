Short: Traffic framing, Space and ⌘-digits

The agent traffic stage no longer wastes a large display: the overview zoom was capped at a flat scale calibrated for a laptop, so on a 4K screen the whole graph sat at laptop size in a stage more than twice as wide, and Follow centred on one exchange with a blank band above it while the rest of the graph hung off the edge. The overview cap now scales with the stage, and Follow keeps its calibrated size but never frames past the edge of the scene. Space now plays and pauses the replay from anywhere on the stage, leaving the key to search fields, focused buttons and open dropdowns. And a project switch (⌘1–9, ⌘0, the `g` chords, the palette) closes the traffic log instead of navigating behind it, which made those shortcuts read as dead.
