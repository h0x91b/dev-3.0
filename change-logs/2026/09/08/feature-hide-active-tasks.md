Short: Hide tasks from the sidebar

Started a task, then set it aside for something more pressing? Click the eye on its sidebar row to drop it out of Active Tasks until you pick it up again. Its agent keeps running and keeps notifying you; an eye appears next to the sidebar's filter funnel while anything is hidden and brings those rows back, dimmed, until you press it again — with a red dot when one of the hidden tasks is the one asking for you. `is:hidden` finds them from the search box or the filter funnel. Hiding covers every variant of the task.
