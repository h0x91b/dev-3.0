Short: Agent traffic gains a 3D stage

Agent traffic gains a third presentation, Experiment 3, drawing the same recorded traffic as a 3D scene: one hexagonal platform per project, a machine per task whose shape comes from its recorded type (coordinator, reviewer, worker), and beams between them carrying the delivery verdict — a thin line for delivered, dashed with stop plates for held and scheduled, gapped and crossed for never delivered. Three scene layers answer three questions about the same rows: Situation, Waiting and Traffic. The stage carries its own scene-mode switch, metric strip, activity panel listing what is actually waiting on you, legend and camera controls; drag orbits, scroll zooms, F frames the selection and H frames everything. It inherits the replay transport, the filters and the inspector from the screen, so scrubbing rebuilds card states from recorded board moves exactly as Experiment 2 does and a card with no record stays neutral. Experiment 2 remains the default and Experiments 1 and 2 are untouched. Where WebGL 2 is unavailable the stage says so and the message list keeps working. Picking a presentation is now one dropdown in the toolbar instead of a row of buttons, and its list finally says in a sentence what each presentation draws.
