Short: Replay rebuilds past board state

Agent traffic replay now walks a timeline of recorded board movements, messages and notifications instead of an index into messages, so a task appears at its recorded creation and changes status at the moment it actually moved — even in an interval where nothing was said. A finished card carries a readable stamp with its own icon and word. A task whose past was never recorded renders as unknown rather than showing today's status, hibernation and project stay flagged as current-only, and scrubbing backwards reverses everything exactly because the projection is a pure function of the cursor.
