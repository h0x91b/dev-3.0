Short: See when you own completion

The completion-ownership control in the task bar now reads as a deliberate mode instead of another blue toggle: while it is on the chip turns amber, spells out "I'll complete it myself" and fills the owner badge on its icon, while off stays a quiet grey person. The tooltip, the accessible name and the phone's action row all say the consequence — dev3 will not suggest completing the task after a merge — so the missing prompt no longer looks like a bug.
