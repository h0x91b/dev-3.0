Short: Drag a time range on the traffic timeline

The Agent traffic replay transport now carries a time ruler under its track: drag across it to select an interval, drag the band to move that interval through history, or drag either end to grow and shrink it, with the arrow keys doing the same from the keyboard. A dragged interval is the same window the Last hour / Last 24 hours presets and the calendar day write, so picking a preset simply replaces it and Live returns to the rolling 24 hours.
