Short: Traffic cards arc to their new column

A task card in Agent traffic no longer snaps to its new status: the status colour fades over 700ms across the whole card — wash, top rule, rail, dot, stamp and the zoomed-out cell fill — the status word cross-fades over the one it replaces, and the card flies to its new slot along an arc instead of teleporting, since that slot is its Kanban column — over the top when it heads left, under the bottom when it heads right, round the left going up and round the right going down, so two cards trading places pass on opposite sides. Cards slide only while the scene holds the same cards, so a filter change never sets the whole graph gliding. With reduced motion on, the new status is simply there.
