Short: Traffic cards move and fade between statuses

A task card in Agent traffic no longer snaps to its new status: the status colour fades over 700ms across the whole card — wash, top rule, rail, dot, stamp and the zoomed-out cell fill — the status word cross-fades over the one it replaces, and the card travels to its new slot instead of teleporting, since that slot is its Kanban column. Cards slide only while the scene holds the same cards, so a filter change never sets the whole graph gliding. With reduced motion on, the new status is simply there.
