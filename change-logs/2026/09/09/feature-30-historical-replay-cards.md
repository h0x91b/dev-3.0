Short: Replay shows past task states

Agent traffic replay now shows the board as it was, not as it is: while the cursor stands in the past, each card carries the status and column it had at that instant, rebuilt from recorded board moves, and a task appears on the stage at the moment it was created instead of sitting there from the start. Recorded board moves are replay steps of their own, so an hour where tasks moved and nobody wrote anything still plays — the transport names each step ("Task created", "Board move"), and the lit wire and its subject stay on the last real message rather than blanking. A card whose past was never recorded says "Status not recorded" and stays neutral instead of borrowing today's colour, one whose earliest moves have been dropped says so, and completed or cancelled cards carry a stamp with an icon and the word rather than colour alone. Hibernation, project and variant are not recorded anywhere, so the stage says once that those stay current.
