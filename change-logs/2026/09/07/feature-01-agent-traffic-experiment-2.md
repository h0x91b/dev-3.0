Short: Replay traffic on connected cards

Agent traffic defaults to Experiment 2 with named project blocks, five-card rows below each coordinator, message replay, calibrated 0.25×–8× speed/step controls starting at a calmer 1×, Follow/Focus and draggable minimap navigation; wires avoid cards and carry violet drops (red on failure), and messages without a sender task reveal their recipient with a text-only speech bubble. Follow frames each exchange once, returns to the whole scene when idle or replay ends, and starts with the last 24 hours; a calendar selects earlier local days and loads their retained messages automatically. Completed tasks stay marked and hibernated tasks remain grey; the inspector opens when needed, Experiment 1 remains available, and the separate Settings toggle controls the whole feature.
