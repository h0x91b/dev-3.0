Short: No stray artifact popup on task switch

An artifact panel left open in one task no longer reappears as a centred popup over an unrelated board when you switch tasks or projects — it waits hidden and comes back, with its document, version and unsent draft intact, when you return to that task. Artifacts opened from the archived task modal, from a toast for a task that is not on screen, or with "Open artifacts in a popup" on are unaffected.
