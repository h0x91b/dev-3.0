Short: Launch dialog waits once you touch it

The "Agent wants to start this task" dialog no longer starts the task on its own once you have touched it: the first click or key press cancels the countdown for good, on the timer that actually launches, and the dialog then waits for an explicit Launch or Decline. The unattended countdown now defaults to 60 seconds instead of 5 minutes, and a configured delay (or "Never") is untouched.
