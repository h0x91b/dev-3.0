Short: Pipeline ring with one-click complete

The task card's seven-dot pipeline is now a compact progress ring carrying the stage number, which frees roughly a third of the status row, and a ✓ next to it sends the task straight to Completed after a single in-app confirmation. That dialog opens instantly and streams a local-only check for unsaved work into itself, instead of waiting seconds on git fetches. The "Move to" menu gained a ring header with the current stage, a promoted Completed row, and larger, better-aligned rows.
