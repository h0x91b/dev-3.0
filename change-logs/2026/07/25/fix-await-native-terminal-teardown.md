Completing, cancelling, aborting preparation, or deleting a task on the native terminal backend now waits until that task's terminal host, shell, and descendants are verified gone before the cleanup script runs and the worktree is removed. If the stop cannot be confirmed, teardown aborts with an actionable error and leaves the worktree and task state intact for a retry, while tasks on the legacy tmux backend keep their previous non-blocking teardown.
