The isolated native terminal registry now classifies persisted session records explicitly as attachable, lost (host gone or PID reused), or unreadable, each with an actionable diagnostic, and exposes it through `inspectRecovery`/`recoverSessions`, `status()`, and the dev-only `cli.ts recover [--cleanup]`. Cleanup stays token-matched and passive: it removes only verified native metadata, never signals a reused PID, never starts a replacement shell, and never touches tmux state.
