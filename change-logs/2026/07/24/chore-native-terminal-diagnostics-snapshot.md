Added a read-only native terminal diagnostics module (src/bun/native-terminal-diagnostics/) that shapes injected registry read-API data into a versioned, JSON-safe snapshot plus a compact human-readable formatter. It reports host/session facts and bounded queue/parser counters, marks unavailable facts as explicit "unknown" with a reason, and redacts endpoint, token, command, and output by construction. Internal groundwork for the tmux-removal roadmap with no product caller yet.
