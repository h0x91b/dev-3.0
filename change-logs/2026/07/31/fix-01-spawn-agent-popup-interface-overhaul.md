Short: Launch dialogs work from the keyboard

The dialogs that pick which agent, model and account to launch are now fully keyboard-operable: the Provider/Model/Mode dropdowns, the favorites menu and the account switcher are reachable by Tab and driven by arrow keys, and Escape closes the open dropdown instead of throwing away the whole launch configuration. The account popover opens below its trigger instead of covering the dialog, states which account is selected to screen readers, and says whether a quota reading is used or remaining. Field labels appear once per list rather than on every variant row, the favorites star no longer stretches across the dialog at narrow widths, Enter respects the "Launch later" panel, launch failures announce themselves with a recovery hint, and small print in the account popover meets contrast in light theme.
