Short: Crisp header under iPhone status bar

In remote mode on iOS 26 and later, Safari no longer blurs and washes out the top breadcrumb header beneath its Liquid Glass status bar. On mobile browsers, the header now sits in a sticky, full-width, opaque base-surface top band without a backdrop filter, which triggers WebKit's fixed-colour extension and hides the top scroll-edge blur while keeping the glass header on desktop.
