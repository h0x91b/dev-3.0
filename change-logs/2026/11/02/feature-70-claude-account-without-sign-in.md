Short: Add Claude account without signing in

Add account now asks first (Sign in, Cancel, and for Claude "Add without signing in") and creates or runs nothing until you choose. "Add without signing in" keeps the account's folder with no identity and shows the command to sign in later (the account's name becomes its email once a login appears), for setups where a wrapper owns the credentials. A custom Claude executable now fails the launch with a clear error instead of silently running the system `claude` (its path can now be set or reset even when `claude` is installed), and an account that cannot launch fails instead of falling back to the system login.
