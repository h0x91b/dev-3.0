Short: Remote tab asks before closing

In browser remote mode the browser now asks before the dev3 tab closes or navigates away, so a stray Ctrl+W or ⌘W no longer drops the session, and desktop Chrome/Edge enter fullscreen on the first click so Ctrl+W, Ctrl+T and the digit shortcuts reach the app and the terminal (Esc still reaches a focused terminal or an open dialog, and leaving fullscreen keeps you windowed). Both are on by default and can be turned off in Settings → System, and the browser menu bar gained View → Toggle Full Screen.
