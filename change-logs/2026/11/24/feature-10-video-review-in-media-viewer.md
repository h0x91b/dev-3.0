Short: Review agent videos frame by frame

Videos in the shared media viewer now have their own review bar: step frame by frame (, and .), change speed from 0.25× to 2× (< and >), and press Comment (C) to pin a note to the paused moment. Comments show as markers on the timeline that jump back to their moment, survive reopening, and go to the task's agent in one message with exact timestamps and frame numbers.
