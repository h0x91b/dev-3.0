Short: Delete old videos to free disk

Videos and audio an agent showed with `dev3 show-media` can now be deleted to free disk: right-click a clip in the media viewer and choose "Delete video file", or use "Delete older clips" in the task's Media section to remove every clip of 15 MB or more except the newest. The confirmation states how much disk is really freed (a copy of a render that is still on disk frees almost nothing on macOS), and the entry stays in the history as "Removed to free space".
