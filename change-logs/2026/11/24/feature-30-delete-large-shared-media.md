Short: Delete old videos to free disk

Videos and audio an agent showed with `dev3 show-media` can now be deleted to free disk: right-click a clip in the media viewer and choose "Delete video file", or "Free … · delete older clips" (a button at the end of the viewer's thumbnail rail, the same menu, and a finished task's Media section) to remove every clip of 15 MB or more except the newest. The label and the confirmation state how much disk is really freed (a copy of a render that is still on disk frees almost nothing on macOS), and the entry stays in the history as "Removed to free space". Closed tasks now also keep only their newest large clips (15 for a week after closing, then 3, then 1 after 30 days), the 60-day media prune starts at 15 MB instead of 5 MB, and "Keep" in a clip's right-click menu protects it from both.
