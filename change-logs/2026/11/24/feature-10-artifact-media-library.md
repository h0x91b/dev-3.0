Short: Library of every report and media clip

A new Library screen lists every report, video, image and audio clip agents have shown you, across all projects and including finished tasks: search by title, caption, task or agent, filter by type and project, see what arrived today and what you have not opened yet, and see which projects and tasks take the most disk. Open it from the header's More menu, View → Show Library or the command palette; items open in the usual viewers and link back to their task. Select video and audio clips there, even across tasks, and delete their stored files in one go; the confirmation states how much disk that really frees, and each entry stays listed as removed.
