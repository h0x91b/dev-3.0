Short: Artifact controls no longer overlap

Artifact reports no longer let controls run into each other: slider tick labels get their own room, widgets stacked in one card (forms, presets, charts, tables, KPI grids, bare inputs) get a gap, crowded button groups wrap whole buttons instead of breaking words, slider value pills stay on one line, stacked table rows fit a phone at enlarged text, and the demo radar and sankey keep their labels apart.
