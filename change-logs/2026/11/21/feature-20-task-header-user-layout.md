Short: Rearrange the task header

Right-click any task-header control (Shift+F10 from the keyboard, or long-press it on a touch screen) to move it left or right, send it to its bar's More menu, use another control of that bar in its place, or hide it; the layout is the same for every task and survives a restart. The eye button lists every hidden control by bar, and the narrow actions sheet offers the same restore rows for touch.
