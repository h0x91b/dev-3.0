Completing or cancelling a task while its terminal is open no longer flashes a "Terminal session ended" or "Previous agent session found" card in the middle of the screen; the terminal pane keeps the task header and shows the same "Shutting down…" state as the task row until the task closes, and comes back with its recovery options if the teardown fails.
