Short: Completion bar no longer truncates

The "On the task" completion bar stays on one line in every language: a merge offer now reads "Branch merged", and the note, the settings gear and "I'll complete it myself" open in a panel above the bar on hover. When work would be lost, the first Complete click shows the warning and a second one completes; the dark-theme Complete button is readable again, and a merge shows the merge icon instead of a ✓.
