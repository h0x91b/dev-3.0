Short: Number keys follow spaces

A new Keyboard setting, "Number keys follow spaces", makes ⌘1–⌘9 (and ⇧⌘1–⌘9, and `g` + digit) address spaces and standalone projects in one mixed order; a space opens its unified board and ⌘0 stays Operations. With it on, the dashboard shows that same single list with ⌘N hints, and a space can be dragged (or stepped with Move up / Move down) among standalone projects. The order is stored in spaces.json as `mixedOrder`.
