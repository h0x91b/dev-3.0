Short: Complete task asks where you click

Clicking ✓ or Cancel on a board card, an Active Tasks sidebar row or an Activity row now opens the confirmation as a popover beside that button instead of a full-screen dialog, still with the full task card and the unsaved-work warnings, and focus goes back to the button if you keep the task. A merged branch, an agent's request and the open task's own buttons still ask in the dialog.
