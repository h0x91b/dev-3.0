Short: Complete task asks where you click

Clicking ✓ (or Cancel) on a board card, a sidebar row or an Activity row now opens the confirmation as a popover beside that button instead of a full-screen dialog, and Complete or Cancel on the open task asks on the task's completion card over its terminal. Both still show the full task card (number, project, priority, overview, labels) and the unsaved-work warnings, and the full-screen dialog remains only where there is no button and no task view, such as immersive fullscreen.
