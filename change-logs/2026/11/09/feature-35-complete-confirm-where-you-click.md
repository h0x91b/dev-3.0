Short: Complete task asks where you click

Clicking ✓ or Cancel on a task in the Active Tasks sidebar now opens the confirmation as a popover beside that button instead of a full-screen dialog, still with the full task card and the unsaved-work warnings, and focus goes back to the button if you keep the task. Board cards, Activity rows, the open task's own buttons, a merged branch and an agent's request still ask in the dialog.
