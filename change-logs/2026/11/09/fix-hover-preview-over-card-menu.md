Short: No task preview over the card menu

The task hover preview no longer opens while you move through a task card's right-click menu and its Priority, Labels, Move to, Move to project or Open in submenus, on the board and in the Active Tasks sidebar. It also stays closed while any menu or picker is open, so moving off a sidebar row's menu onto a neighbouring row no longer opens that row's preview under the menu.
