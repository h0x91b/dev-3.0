Short: Readable buttons in the dark theme

In the dark theme, white text on the confirm dialog's primary and destructive buttons and on several accent buttons (attention options, secret request, spaces, model catalog) measured 2.3 to 3.0:1, so they read as disabled; they now use the deeper fill colours (5.1 to 6.5:1). A test now fails on white text over a bare accent, danger, success or warning background.
