Short: Suggest hibernating inactive tasks

Dev3 now suggests hibernating waiting tasks after 24 hours of inactivity, with one dialog for all eligible tasks and a 30-minute auto-confirm countdown. Interacting with the dialog stops the countdown; Not now asks again after another inactivity period, and both delays are configurable in Settings → Tasks & Board. Viewing does not reset inactivity; extra terminal panes, working agents, live dev servers and Keep alive tasks stay protected, and unrelated worktree processes are preserved.
