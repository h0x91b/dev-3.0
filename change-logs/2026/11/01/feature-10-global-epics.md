Short: Group tasks across projects with epics

Epics group tasks from any project under one named, coloured effort: the card shows its epic chip (click it to filter the board with the new `epic:` token, with a footnote linking matches on other boards), the task detail and New Task dialog have an epic picker, and Settings → Tasks & Board → Epics renames, recolours and archives them. Tasks created by a task inherit its epic, and `dev3 epic` plus `--epic`/`--no-epic` on `task create`/`task update` and `dev3 tasks list --epic` drive it from the CLI.
