Short: Attention view for project boards

Project and space boards gain an Attention board view that sorts tasks into buckets — Needs you, Ready to start, Stalled and Waiting on PR — each defined by a board search query. Buckets are edited, reordered, added or reset from View options, a task shows in the first bucket it matches, quiet tasks move into Stalled on their own, and on narrow screens the buckets stack.
