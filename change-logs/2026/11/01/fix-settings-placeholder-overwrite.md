Short: Updating no longer resets your settings

Settings are never saved from the screen's pre-load placeholder any more: when the first settings read at launch was lost, opening help mode (or finishing a tour, hiding a control, or changing a setting before Settings loaded) overwrote settings.json, wiping stored settings and switching the default Claude preset to Fable 5 X-High. Saves now always build on the stored settings, and the retired plain Auto/Bypass presets map to the default Opus 5.5 Medium tier.
